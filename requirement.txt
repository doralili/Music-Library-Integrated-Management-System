Database Course Design Requirements

I. Topic: Self-defined (including but not limited to pharmacy management systems, academic information management systems, library management systems, supermarket product sales management systems, medical information management systems, etc.).

II. Requirements:  
Complete the practical training task in groups of 3-4 people. Each group member needs to complete the development task of a specific system function and explain their contribution to that task in the documentation. The detail of the task requirements are as follows (taking the pharmacy management system as an example):
Based on Huawei's openGauss, establish a chain pharmacy management system and its related database. The system users include: system administrators, pharmacy administrators, and salespersons.  
- **System administrators** have the authority to manage (including adding, deleting, modifying, etc.) pharmacy administrators and salespersons.  
- **Pharmacy administrators** are responsible for managing (including adding, deleting, modifying, etc.) drug-related information.  
- **Salespersons** can use the system to search (by entering drug names, manufacturers, or codes) and sell drugs.

III. Submission Files:  
- Source code and executable files of the system (if applicable).  
- A database application system development manual in English (template provided in Attachment 1).  
- Database files and a demonstration video (no longer than 3 minutes).  
Place all files in a folder named "Student Name + Development System Name," compress it, and submit it.

IV. Submission Deadline: Before Friday of Week 15.

V. Grading Criteria:  
1. The database application system development manual accounts for 40 points out of 100, divided as follows:  
   - Project planning: 10 points.  
   - Database design: 20 points.  
   - System development and implementation: 10 points.  

2. System functionality accounts for 60 points out of 100, divided as follows:  
   - User-friendly interface: 10 points.  
   - Data addition, deletion, and modification functions: 10 points each.  
   - Data query function: 10 points (if only exact query is implemented, 5 points will be awarded).  
   - User permission management function: 10 points.
